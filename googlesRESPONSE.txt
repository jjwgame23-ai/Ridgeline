In [Squad](https://www.google.com/search?q=squad&kgmid=/m/013227jm), vehicles are not just transport or power-ups—they are highly valuable, finite team assets that function as mobile fire support, logistics lifelines, and tactical chess pieces [1]. Misusing a vehicle doesn't just lose a firefight; it drains your team's Ticket count and can cost you the match [1].
Vehicles in Squad are strictly categorized by their tactical roles:
------------------------------
## 📦 1. Logistics (Logies) & Transport: The Lifeline
Logistics trucks are arguably the most important vehicles in the game. Without them, your team cannot build spawn points or rearm.

* 
* FOB Deployment: A Squad Leader uses a Logi truck to carry Construction and Ammo supplies to build Forward Operating Bases (FOBs) and HABs (spawn structures).
* Supply Runs: Dedicating a player or small squad to drive Logis back and forth from the main base to active FOBs ensures the frontline never runs out of ammo or defensive resources.
* Stealth Infiltration: Transport trucks and light utility vehicles are often driven off-road, using the terrain to conceal the sound of their engines and drop infantry behind enemy lines for a surprise flank.
* 

## 🛡️ 2. APCs and IFVs: Infantry Support & Mechanized Infantry
Armored Personnel Carriers (APCs like the Stryker or BTR) and Infantry Fighting Vehicles (IFVs like the Bradley or BMP) bridge the gap between transport and heavy armor.

* 
* Mechanized Infantry: A squad rides inside the APC directly into an objective. The vehicle drops the infantry off, then stays nearby to provide heavy fire support using its autocannon.
* Infantry Screening: Vehicles must never enter dense urban areas or forests alone. Infantry must "screen" for the vehicle by clearing out bushes and buildings ahead of it to kill enemy Heavy Anti-Tank (HAT) and Light Anti-Tank (LAT) players before they can fire a rocket.
* Component Targeting: APC and IFV crews actively aim for specific parts of enemy vehicles—shooting the tracks/wheels to immobilize them, or the turret ring to prevent them from firing back.
* 

## 💥 3. Main Battle Tanks (MBTs): Heavy Armor Supremacy
Tanks (like the M1A2 Abrams or T-72B3) rule the open battlefield but require immense coordination.

* 
* Maximum Range Engagement: Tanks excel at sitting hundreds of meters away on hills or ridgelines, utilizing their optics to lock down entire fields, valleys, or roads, completely denying enemy movement.
* Hunter-Killer Pairs: Tanks rarely operate alone. They work with a lighter, faster vehicle (like a Scout Car or IFV) that spots targets for them, or they move in tandem with another tank to instantly overwhelm enemy armor.
* 

## 🚁 4. Helicopters: Rapid Re-positioning and Logistics
Helicopters offer unmatched vertical mobility but are highly vulnerable to anti-aircraft fire and heavy armor.

* 
* The "Air Bridge": Helicopters can drop supplies to remote or surrounded FOBs (like mountaintops) much faster than a ground vehicle.
* Rapid Insertion & Extraction: Dropping a full squad via a landing zone (LZ) directly onto a back-cap point or pulling a squad out of a failed attack to redeploy them across the map instantly.
* Aerial Reconnaissance: Pilots or co-pilots scan the ground from safety, marking enemy FOBs, vehicles, and troop movements directly onto the team map.
* 

------------------------------
## 🚨 The Golden Rules of Squad Vehicle Tactics

* 
* Assets Cost Tickets: Every time a vehicle is destroyed, your team loses valuable tickets (ranging from 5 tickets for a light jeep to 15 tickets for an IFV, and 20 tickets for a Main Battle Tank). Throwing them away carelessly guarantees a loss.
* Turn Off the Engine: When parked near an objective or hidden in a bush, turn the engine off. Vehicle engines in Squad are incredibly loud and will give away your position to enemy infantry blocks away.
* Always Claim Roles: You must have a dedicated Crewman kit and be in a designated squad to operate heavy armor. Never take an armored vehicle without a dedicated driver and gunner—solo-crewing heavy armor is highly ineffective and heavily frowned upon in the community.
